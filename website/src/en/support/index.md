# Support Koharia

Koharia is a community-maintained open-source project.
If it improves your reading experience, you can support its continued maintenance and development through Aifadian.

<a href="https://ifdian.net/a/album-Koharia" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Support Koharia on Aifadian</a>

Support is entirely voluntary and does not affect app features, content access, or issue priority.

You can also help by reporting issues, improving documentation, translating, or contributing code.
See [Contribute](/en/docs/contribute).
