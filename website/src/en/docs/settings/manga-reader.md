# Manga reader

Manga reader settings adjust the reading experience for image-based comics, image folders, comic archives, PDF, and DjVu.

## Reading mode

Choose a reading mode that suits the content and your preference.
You can also combine it with page zoom and interface controls for a more comfortable display.

## Navigation and actions

Adjust page navigation, tap actions, and double-tap animation behavior.
Try options one at a time while reading familiar content, then keep the behavior that feels right for everyday reading.

## Connection-scoped options

Some reader behavior can be configured per connection.
This helps retain a suitable reading experience when you connect multiple Komga servers or local libraries, or when their content types differ substantially.

For EPUB, TXT, and experimental Mobipocket or Kindle text, use [Book reader settings](/en/docs/settings/book-reader).
