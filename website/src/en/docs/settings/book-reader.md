# Book reader

The book reader handles reflowable books from Komga or local libraries.
EPUB is fully supported; local TXT recognizes common encodings, while MOBI, PRC, AZW, and AZW3 have experimental plain-text and basic-metadata support.

## Typography and appearance

Adjust the font, text spacing, and reading background to make long reading sessions more comfortable.
If you read in different environments, review the display in both light and dark appearance.

## Progress and navigation

The book reader supports paged or continuous reading with typography, table of contents, bookmarks, full-text search, and progress features depending on the format.
Supported Komga progress can sync with the relevant server, while local reading positions and bookmarks are kept on the device.

## Cache

EPUB content from Komga can use the book cache according to the app settings.
Caching can reduce repeated loading and dependence on the network, but it also uses device storage.

For saving Komga content for offline use, also see [Download settings](/en/docs/settings/downloads).
