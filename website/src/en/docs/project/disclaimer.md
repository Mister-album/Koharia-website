# Disclaimer

Koharia is a community-maintained, third-party open-source Android reader.
It connects to Komga servers that you configure and scans or imports local folders that you explicitly authorize.
Koharia is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a service of Komga.

## Content and authorization

Koharia does not provide, host, or distribute media content; its local index is used only to organize and display authorized files on the device.
You are responsible for ensuring that the content you access, download, or save with Koharia, and how you use it, comply with applicable laws and the rights holder's authorization.

## Servers, accounts, and local files

You are responsible for your Komga server, network exposure, account permissions, and API keys.
Do not include server addresses, passwords, tokens, or other sensitive data in public issues, screenshots, or logs.
Manage the folder access granted to Koharia carefully, and back up important files and reading records before changing metadata storage, moving files, or clearing app data.

## Software status

Koharia is provided "as is", without a promise that it is fit for a particular purpose or always available and error-free.
Back up important data and consider the impact on reading records and downloads before upgrading, clearing data, or changing your server.

For information about personal data and network requests, see the [Privacy policy](/en/privacy/).
